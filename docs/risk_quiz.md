# Risk quiz (Panel 4)
Three questions decide the user's risk profile. Each answer scores 0, 1 or 2 points.

## Questions

**1. When will you need most of this money?**
- In less than 5 years (0)
- In 5 to 15 years (1)
- In more than 15 years (2)

**2. Your savings drop 20% in one bad year. What do you do?**
- Sell, so I don't lose any more (0)
- Hold on and wait for them to recover (1)
- Invest more while prices are low (2)

**3. Which matters most to you?**
- Not losing the money I put in (0)
- A balance of safety and growth (1)
- The highest growth, regardless of short-term losses(2) 

## Scoring

| Total points | Profile |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Cautious |
| 3–4 | Balanced |
| 5–6 | Adventurous |

## What each profile means in the simulation

| Profile | Expected return (per year) | Volatility (per year) |
|---|---|---|
| Cautious | 4.5% | 7% |
| Balanced | 6.5% | 11% |
| Adventurous | 8.5% | 16% |
